DIGITAL BUSINESS PLAYBOOK
=========================

WEEK 1: FOUNDATION
------------------
1) Buy a simple domain
2) Create one-page website
3) Set up WhatsApp Business profile

WEEK 2: OFFER + PRICING
-----------------------
1) Define your top 3 services/products
2) Set clear starting prices
3) Make one offer customers can understand in 10 seconds

WEEK 3: LEAD CAPTURE
--------------------
1) Add enquiry form
2) Add call and WhatsApp buttons
3) Create follow-up message templates

WEEK 4: VISIBILITY
------------------
1) Post 3 useful social posts per week
2) Share one customer proof weekly
3) Ask every happy customer for review

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
------------------------
- No clear CTA
- No pricing clarity
- Slow response to enquiries
- Over-designing, under-selling

ONE-PAGE DAILY CHECKLIST
------------------------
[ ] Replied to all leads
[ ] Updated one product/service
[ ] Posted one useful content piece
[ ] Checked enquiries and conversions
