CREATOR LANDING KIT
===================

Use this guide to launch a clean, high-converting landing page in one day.

SECTION ORDER
-------------
1) Hero: One clear promise + one CTA
2) Problem: What pain you solve
3) Solution: Your process/service
4) Proof: Testimonials or results
5) Pricing: 2-3 plans max
6) FAQ: Remove buying doubts
7) CTA: Repeat offer with urgency

HERO TEMPLATE
-------------
Headline: "Get [result] without [pain]."
Subtext: "We help [audience] achieve [result] through [method]."
Primary CTA: "Book a free call"
Secondary CTA: "See case studies"

PRICING BLOCK TEMPLATE
----------------------
Plan Name:
Best For:
What's Included:
Price:
CTA:

MOBILE CHECKLIST
----------------
- Headline readable in 2 lines
- CTA button visible without zoom
- Form has max 5 fields
- Contact links tappable

COPY QUICK RULES
----------------
- Use short sentences
- Show benefit before features
- One message per section
- Repeat CTA every 2 sections

DELIVERABLE NOTE
----------------
This starter file gives structure + copy prompts.
Use it with your design file (Figma/Webflow/Framer).
